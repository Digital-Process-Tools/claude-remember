#816 fixed the silent-coercion shape at exactly two call sites --
CONSOLIDATE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS in scripts/run-consolidation.sh and NDC_TIMEOUT_SECONDS in
scripts/save-session.sh -- by adding a `case "$VALUE" in ''|*[!0-9]*) log "WARNING: ... -- using
default N"; VALUE=N ;; esac` guard after each `config ".thresholds.<key>" <default>` read. The
manager's own review of PR #821 counted nine `config ".thresholds.*"` reads across scripts/, of
which only these two now carry that guard. The other seven -- including
`CONSOLIDATE_MAX_BYTES=$(config ".thresholds.consolidate_max_bytes" 600000)`
(scripts/run-consolidation.sh:132, immediately above the now-guarded
CONSOLIDATE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS read) and `MIN_HUMAN=$(config ".thresholds.min_human_messages" 3)`
(scripts/save-session.sh:548) -- still substitute their bare default the instant the config value
is empty or non-numeric, with nothing logged. An operator who typo's
`thresholds.min_human_messages` sees exactly the behaviour of a deliberate default, the same
#816 itself was filed to fix for the two timeout keys.

Ranked non-blocking (`misreports`) by the manager's own findings table: it does not block a
release, and #816's own fix is not wrong for stopping at the two keys its own issue named -- this
is a generalisation of the same shape to sibling call sites, not a defect in #816's diff. Not
filed as a tracker issue for the same reason -- it is a small, mechanical repeat of #816's own
fix shape across a handful of call sites, and /oss:curate is better placed to decide whether it
becomes a rule (e.g. "every `config ".thresholds.*"` numeric read gets the guard") or a single
follow-up issue enumerating the seven sites, than to have this review spawn open one now.
