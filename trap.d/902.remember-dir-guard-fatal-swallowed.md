pinned to match, test_remember_dir_guard_902.py:352). log.sh:1150 appends to

-- "nothing happened" and "refused to save" look identical. What would settle it:

channel the user can read (e.g. hook-errors.log, or stderr with the swallow
removed for FATAL lines specifically).
