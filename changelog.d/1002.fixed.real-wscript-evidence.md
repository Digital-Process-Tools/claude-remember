- Windows: the PostToolUse hidden-launch route added for #1002 had two REASONED-only gaps a stub-`wscript.exe` CI leg could not see -- a `bash -c` script with embedded double quotes whose survival across the exec->CreateProcess->WSH argv hop was never confirmed on a real Windows host, and an unconditional success return after backgrounding `wscript.exe` that swallowed a Windows-Script-Host-disabled launch failure with no fallback. #1002's round 2 removes the embedded-quote argv entry entirely (the outfile redirect now happens inside `scripts/lib-detach-pidwrap.sh` itself, as its own argv entry, rather than as a quoted string another shell re-parses) and runs `wscript.exe` in the foreground so a launch failure now reaches the caller and triggers the existing nohup fallback instead of a silent skip. `tests/test_windows_real_wscript_1002.py` drives the real launcher against the real `wscript.exe` on the `windows-latest` CI legs, including a path with a space and WSH disabled via the registry.
