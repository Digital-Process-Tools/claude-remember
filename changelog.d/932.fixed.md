- `60-git-reconcile.sh` only checked consolidation's lock before fast-forwarding or
  rebasing `now.md` -- not `save-session.sh`'s own lock, which NDC compression commits
  under. A reconcile that landed while NDC was mid-commit could truncate `now.md` at an
  offset NDC's own size/generation guards had no way to know was now stale, dropping
  entries from a second machine before they were ever summarized into `today-*.md`.
  Reconcile now holds save-session.sh's lock dir for the whole fetch/merge/rebase too,
  and declines (retrying next save) exactly as it already did for consolidation's lock
  (#932).
