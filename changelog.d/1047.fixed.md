- Fixed #1047: a `SessionStart` hook handed more than a few kilobytes on stdin — a host that pipes its own transcript in, for instance; 6.8 MB was observed — could sit in Remember's own JSON field extraction for hours, and could not be stopped with an ordinary terminate signal, only a forced kill. It now keeps at most a few kilobytes of the payload, which is all it ever reads from (an id, a couple of paths, the source). Every ordinary payload arrives exactly as it did before, and the hook's own startup cost is unchanged.
